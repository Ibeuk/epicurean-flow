About Me
Food has always been the heartbeat of my life. I left a job I loved at KLM Royal Dutch Airlines to chase my true passion: cooking. Although my position offered financial security and a successful career in business—built after earning a degree in business management and real estate—nothing could compare to the joy of creating delicious meals. As a child, I was captivated by my mother, a gourmet cook, whose kitchen was a realm of transformation, turning simple ingredients into unforgettable culinary experiences.
Twenty years ago, I made a bold decision to trade my corporate attire for a chef’s jacket. I enrolled at the Culinary Institute of America for comprehensive professional chef training, where I gained the foundation necessary to build a food business. My culinary education was further enriched by the chefs I worked alongside in renowned restaurants across Europe and the talented culinary artists I met during my world travels, who generously shared their culinary secrets. My journey eventually took me to the high seas, where I served as an executive chef on a mega yacht, crafting exquisite meals for high profile guests while mastering precision and adaptability.
In Cannes, I founded La Serviette Blanche, where I taught cooking classes, curated culinary experiences, and opened a private dining restaurant. Our cooking classes earned several awards of excellence, and in 2014, the restaurant was honored with a prestigious Michelin accreditation, a testament to our unwavering commitment to exceptional food and service. Later, I established Born to Taste Amsterdam as a subsidiary of La Serviette Blanche, continuing to share my love for teaching and empowering others in the kitchen.
However, a serious shoulder injury forced me to step back from the physical demands of being a chef. At the same time, I was diagnosed with diabetes, which transformed my relationship with food. This personal experience inspired me to create a special division dedicated to diabetes-friendly cooking. As a diabetes patient myself, I’ve learned to adapt beloved recipes into guilt-free delights that do not sacrifice flavor. My mission is to empower others living with diabetes to enjoy delicious meals while maintaining their health.
Embracing this new chapter, I pursued intensive training in content marketing and content creation, equipping myself with the skills to share my culinary journey and insights with a broader audience. With Epicurean Flow, I blend my culinary expertise with engaging content, crafting narratives that inspire others to explore the rich tapestry of flavors and experiences that food has to offer.
Join me on this flavorful adventure, where we celebrate the love of food, the joy of cooking, and the empowerment of creating delicious, diabetes-friendly meals without compromise!

Learn From Experience

Meet Chef Eliane Muskus

Chef Eliane’s culinary path is defined by bold transformation, international expertise, and a commitment to flavor-first cooking.

Holding a degree in Business Development and a corporate career background, Chef Eliane took a bold leap to pursue her lifelong culinary calling. She founded and operated a Michelin-accredited restaurant in Cannes, France, cultivating a mastery of French technique, Mediterranean produce, and precision plating.

Living with diabetes herself, Chef Eliane brings a personal, deeply practical perspective to health-conscious cooking—crafting courses and cookbooks that deliver restaurant-quality dishes without compromise.

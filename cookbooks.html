Digital Cookbook FAQ

How and when do I receive my cookbooks?

Immediately upon successful Wix checkout, your download link is displayed on screen and automatically emailed to you. You can download the PDF file directly to your phone, tablet, or laptop.

Are these recipes suitable for beginners?

Yes! Chef Eliane breaks down Michelin-quality technique into intuitive, manageable steps. Whether you have never toasted whole spices before or cook daily, you will learn the "why" behind each ingredient.

Can I print the cookbooks at home?

Absolutely. All files are high-resolution PDF documents configured for standard A4 and US Letter printing, with dedicated margins for kitchen binders.

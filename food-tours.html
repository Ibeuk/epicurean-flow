Boutique Food Tours & Culinary Walks

Experience European food capitals through the eyes of a professional chef. Discover hidden neighborhood artisans, historic spice docks, and authentic market tastings in Amsterdam and Paris.

Small-group and private culinary journeys designed for passionate food lovers and curious travelers.

Amsterdam
⏱ 3.5 Hours 👥 Max 8 Guests 🍷 7 Tastings

Historic Jordaan & Spice Heritage Walk

Trace Amsterdam’s historic 17th-century spice trading routes. Visit heritage cheese affineurs, taste fresh warm stroopwafels, sample artisanal pickled herring, and savor rich Indonesian satay pairings.

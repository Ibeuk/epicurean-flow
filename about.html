A Legacy of Excellence

About Chef Eliane Muskus

From corporate business development to upstate New York culinary education, Mediterranean mega-yachts, and Cannes Michelin accreditation — a journey of bold transformation and culinary mastery.

Founder & Executive Chef

Trading the Boardroom for the Kitchen

Chef Eliane Muskus’s journey is one of bold transformation, unwavering passion, and culinary mastery. With a degree in Business Development and a thriving corporate career, she took a leap of faith to follow her true calling. Trading the boardroom for the kitchen, she enrolled in a prestigious culinary school in upstate New York, where she refined her skills and discovered her lifelong love for creating extraordinary flavors.

After completing her culinary education in New York, Eliane returned to France, where she served as head chef on a mega yacht in the Mediterranean, catering to high-profile international clients. Her culinary journey subsequently took her through Switzerland, the UK, Italy, Belgium, the Netherlands, and Curaçao.

Following her tenure on the mega yacht, she established her thriving culinary enterprise in Cannes, France—offering acclaimed cooking masterclasses, curated food tours, catering services, sommelier wine and cheese tastings, and a prestigious chef's table restaurant.

Career Milestones

A Career Defined by Excellence

Chef Eliane’s journey is marked by prestigious milestones and international recognition across 23 years in hospitality.

2014

Michelin Accreditation

In 2014, Chef Eliane's chef's table restaurant in Cannes, France, received prestigious Michelin accreditation, celebrating her innovative Mediterranean menu and intimate culinary theater.

500+

Students Mentored

For over two decades, Chef Eliane has taught over 500 students—from enthusiastic home cooks to professional yacht chefs—turning first-time guests into lifelong kitchen masters.

23 Yrs

Culinary Mastery

Five years ago, Eliane relocated from Cannes to Amsterdam, establishing a thriving studio in the Zuidas district. After 23 years in demanding kitchens, she launched Epicurean Flow to share her wisdom globally.

Mission & Innovation

The Modern Mission: Epicurean Flow

Today, Chef Eliane brings her expertise to a global stage through Epicurean Flow, an interactive culinary platform designed to make cooking exciting, healthy, and accessible. With step-by-step video tutorials, fun challenges, mini quizzes, and inspiring anecdotes, Epicurean Flow transforms cooking into an engaging, rewarding experience.

Following her own diagnosis with Type 2 Diabetes five years ago, Chef Eliane also pioneered a new frontier in diabetic cuisine: proving through Michelin techniques that low-carb living can be indulgent, aromatic, and deeply satisfying.

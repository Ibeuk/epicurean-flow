Chef Eliane’s Personal Journey with Type 2 Diabetes

Chef Eliane, a multiple award-winning chef with Michelin accreditation, was diagnosed with Type 2 Diabetes over 8 years ago. Recognizing the challenges ahead, she remains under regular medical supervision and is committed to continuously learning and staying informed on how to manage life with diabetes.

Now retired from commercial restaurant kitchens, Chef Eliane channels over 8 years of personal experience and decades of culinary mastery to craft healthy, flavorful recipes that even non-diabetics savor. Her innovative approach proves that managing diabetes doesn’t have to mean sacrificing taste, joy, or dining pleasure.

Chef’s Signature Creation

Healthy Banana Cake Recipe for Diabetics

Low-sugar, nutrient-dense, and utterly guilt-free. Created specifically for balanced blood glucose without compromising golden texture or warmth.

Prep Time 15 mins
Bake Time 40 mins
Glycemic Impact Low GI
Net Carbs 8g / slice

Ingredients

• 2 ripe medium bananas, mashed with fork
• 2 cups extra-fine almond flour (blanched)
• 3 large pasture-raised eggs at room temp
• 1/4 cup extra virgin coconut oil or grass-fed butter
• 1 tbsp pure Ceylon cinnamon (helps glucose control)
• 1 tsp aluminum-free baking powder
• 1/2 tsp pure vanilla bean extract
• 1/4 cup chopped raw walnuts for crunch
• Pinch of sea salt flakes

Chef's Method

01. Prep & Oven

Preheat your oven to 175°C (350°F). Line an 8x4-inch loaf pan with unbleached parchment paper, leaving a 1-inch overhang on the sides for easy lifting.

02. Combine Wet Ingredients

In a large ceramic mixing bowl, whisk the eggs, melted coconut oil, and vanilla extract until frothy. Gently fold in the mashed ripe bananas.

03. Sift & Fold Dry Elements

Sift the almond flour, Ceylon cinnamon, baking powder, and pinch of sea salt into the bowl. Fold gently with a spatula until just incorporated. Fold in chopped walnuts.

04. Bake to Golden Perfection

Pour batter into the prepared pan. Bake for 38–42 minutes until the top is golden brown and a tester inserted into the center emerges clean. Cool on a wire rack before slicing.

Low-Carb Gourmet Banana Cake for Diabetics

A revolutionary dessert recipe formulated by Chef Eliane that delivers moist texture, warm caramelized sweetness, and fragrant cinnamon spice while keeping blood sugar curves gently balanced.

Blood Sugar Balanced

Moist Almond & Spiced Banana Cake

⏱ Prep: 15 mins
🔥 Bake: 35 mins
🍰 Yield: 8 Slices
📉 Net Carbs: 6g / slice

Ingredients

▪ 2 medium semi-ripe bananas (green-tipped for lower fructose)
▪ 200g (2 cups) ultra-fine blanched almond flour
▪ 3 large pasture-raised eggs, room temperature
▪ 60ml (1/4 cup) melted cold-pressed coconut oil or ghee
▪ 2 tbsp natural granulated erythritol or allulose
▪ 1.5 tsp fresh ground Ceylon cinnamon
▪ 1 tsp aluminum-free baking powder + pinch of sea salt
▪ 1 tsp pure Madagascar vanilla bean paste
Chef Eliane's Tip:

Always choose bananas with slight green on the tips. They contain high levels of resistant starch which feeds gut flora without causing insulin spikes!

Culinary Method

Step 01
Preheat & Tin Preparation

Preheat your oven to 175°C (350°F). Line an 8-inch loaf tin with unbleached parchment paper, leaving a 1-inch overhang on the sides for easy lifting after baking.

Step 02
Emulsify Wet Base

In a large ceramic mixing bowl, mash the bananas thoroughly with a fork until smooth. Whisk in the 3 eggs, melted coconut oil, and vanilla paste until glossy and homogenous.

Step 03
Incorporate Spiced Dry Ingredients

Gently fold in the almond flour, ground Ceylon cinnamon, baking powder, sweetener, and sea salt with a silicone spatula. Stir just until combined—do not overmix.

Step 04
Bake & Cool to Set Crumb

Pour the batter into the prepared tin. Bake for 32–36 minutes until a wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean and the crust is deep golden. Allow to cool completely for 20 minutes before slicing to let the almond crumb structure firmly set.

Healthy Banana Cake Recipe for Diabetics

A wholesome, naturally sweet banana bread crafted with blanched almond flour, Ceylon cinnamon, pasture-raised eggs, and raw walnuts. Completely free of refined sugars and processed flours.

Key Ingredients:

• 2 ripe medium bananas
• 2 cups extra-fine almond flour
• 3 large eggs at room temp
• 1/4 cup extra virgin coconut oil
• 1 tbsp pure Ceylon cinnamon
• 1/4 cup chopped raw walnuts

Chef's Executive Summary

Step 1

Mash ripe bananas and whisk thoroughly with pasture eggs and melted coconut oil.

Step 2

Sift almond flour with Ceylon cinnamon and baking powder; fold into wet mixture with raw walnuts.

Step 3

Bake at 175°C (350°F) for 40 minutes until golden brown with a tender crumb.
